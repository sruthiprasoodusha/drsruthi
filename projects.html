Fiber Optic Biosensor Array Platform for Food Toxin Detection

Design and fabrication of a dip type, label-free localized surface plasmon resonance based fiber optic immunosensor to detect ultra-low concentrations of Ochratoxin-A in food. A compact U-bent optical fiber sensor functionalized employing metallic nanoparticle and composite solution of antibody encapsulated in metal organic framework demonstrated the utilization of sensor probe in rapidly detecting OTA traces in grape juice, for which a patent is filed.

Key ideas

  • Attomolar analyte sensing technology development.
  • Industrial analysis of food toxins with portable sensors.
  • Patent
